📖 User Guide — Central Kitchen Dashboard

A quick guide to using this dashboard on your phone or computer. It shows real Central Kitchen data pulled from the ERP, PKT warehouse, and sales-order exports.

🔐 Signing in

1. Open the site and enter your email & password.
2. You stay signed in while you use it. For security, you are signed out automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity or when you close the tab.
3. Each person signs in with their own account — the name shown next to “uploaded by” comes from your account.

🧭 The tabs

Dashboard — the main view: production, variance, logistics, demand.
Details — every raw material in a searchable, exportable table.
Recipes — the standard recipes (per batch and per unit).
Guide — this page.

⬆️ Uploading data

Use Upload ERP data on the Dashboard. Drag or choose the Excel exports and press Process files. The system detects each file automatically — you can select all of them together in one go:
• Kitchen (CK) — 4 files from Codemax:
  – REPORT_Stock_Movement_Details_(Raw_Materials)
  – REPORT_Stock_Balance_(Stock_Value_By_Date)_(Raw_Materials)
  – REPORT_Job_Production_Variance_(Output)
  – REPORT_Wastage_Reports
  – REPORT_Job_Production_Material_Costing (for production cost variance)
• PKT (USFOOD): Frozen + Ambient — Stock Movement + Stock Balance (daily)
• Demand: REPORT_Sales_Order_Listing (By Delivery Date or By Created Date — full outlet demand)
Stock Balance is a snapshot, not a daily log — the system only keeps the earliest upload as the opening balance and the latest upload as the closing balance. Once an opening balance is set, you only need to upload the most recent Stock Balance each time (not every day in between) for the closing side to stay current.
Data accumulates — new uploads add to what is there, they do not wipe it. Everything is saved to the shared database, so anyone who signs in sees the same up-to-date data on any device.

📅 Choosing a period

Use Period (This week / This month / All) or set a Custom range with the two date boxes and press Apply. The dashboard cards update to that period. It opens on the current month by default.

📊 What each card means

Top metrics — outlets ordering (from Sales Orders), products made, average waste %, variance alerts, and PKT units dispatched.
Production output — planned vs actual for each Central Kitchen product.
Yield & waste — dough yield % and waste % by size.
Usage variance alerts (CK GRN) — how actual raw-material usage compares to the recipe standard. Red = large over-usage, amber = moderate. Large outliers are flags to check, not confirmed loss.
Weekly auto-order — items running low (under ~1.5 weeks of stock).
Cost overview — stock value on hand, raw consumed, and waste cost (RM).
Wastage detail — waste by item and by cause (e.g. spoiled).
Month-over-month — this month vs last month for production, waste, and cost, with ▲▼ arrows.
PKT Logistics — dispatches from the USFOOD warehouse to outlets. Use the Malaysia / Singapore toggle to switch region (kept separate). Includes warehouse stock on-hand and a full dispatch log.
Outlet Demand — what outlets ordered from the Central Kitchen (from sales orders).
Production cost variance (Job Costing) — for each product it works out the typical cost of raw material per unit produced (the median across all jobs), then flags any job that cost 25% or more above/below that norm. High numbers = a batch that used more material than usual, worth checking. Uses cost in RM so mixed units (PCS/KG/CTN) don't distort it.
Production vs Demand — for each CK product, compares units produced (Job Production) against units ordered by outlets (Sales Orders) for the month, over the dates both reports cover. Demand is converted to the production unit using pack-size. Coverage near 100% = on target; well below = may run short; well above = possible excess. Needs both the Job Production and Sales Order reports uploaded.
Weekly trend — production, waste, or variance over the weeks.

🔍 Search & ⬇ Export

The Details, PKT, Outlet Demand, Job Costing and Production vs Demand sections each have a search box to filter quickly, and an Export CSV button to download the data for Excel.
Use 🖨 Summary / PDF (top of the Dashboard) to open a clean one-page summary you can print or save as PDF.

ℹ️ Good to know

• Kitchen (CK) data is Malaysia only. PKT keeps MY and SG separate.
• Recipe-variance uses the Codemax recipe standard matched by item code — treat big numbers as items to verify.
• Outlet demand reflects only the sales orders uploaded, not necessarily every outlet.
• To keep the dashboard current, upload the latest exports regularly (e.g. PKT daily).
